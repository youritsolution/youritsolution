Privacy Policy

SABSorter helps staff place a shared iPad into the correct Jamf School location. It is configured entirely by your organisation's MDM and has no user accounts. SABSorter does not collect, sell, or share personal information, and the developer never receives your data — the app communicates only with the Jamf School instance your organisation configures.

Last updated: June 11, 2026

How SABSorter Is Configured

SABSorter is a managed app. All of its settings arrive through Managed App Configuration that your organisation pushes from Jamf School (the MDM). There is no sign-up, no login, and no account. The app reads the device serial number, the device name, an optional location label, and — for sorting campaigns only — the connection details for your Jamf School instance.

QR Mode — No Network Access

In its default mode, SABSorter simply displays the device's serial number as a QR code together with a location label. In this mode the app makes no network requests and holds no credentials. Nothing leaves the device. An administrator scans the code from their own device to perform the sorting.

Self-Service Mode — What the App Accesses

When your organisation includes API connection details in the configuration, SABSorter can move the device itself. In this mode it connects only to the Jamf School instance your organisation configured, over HTTPS, to:

  • look up this device by its serial number to obtain its identifier and current location
  • retrieve the list of locations so a staff member can choose one
  • optionally, when user lookup is enabled, read the directory to match a typed username to a location
  • move ("migrate") this device to the chosen location

The data involved — device serial number, device identifier (UDID), device name, location names and identifiers, and (if user lookup is enabled) usernames — is device and location metadata that already lives in your organisation's Jamf School instance.

API Credentials

In self-service mode the app uses a scoped API key supplied by your organisation through the MDM configuration. The key is used solely to authenticate to the configured Jamf School instance and is never sent anywhere else. Where it is supplied in encrypted form, it is decrypted on-device only to make those calls. SABSorter does not ask the user for any password or credential.

What SABSorter Does Not Collect

SABSorter contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking frameworks, and no third-party SDKs. It does not collect your name, email, photos, contacts, calendar, GPS location, advertising identifiers, usage analytics, or crash reports. The developer (Your IT Solution) does not receive, store, or have access to any data from the app — all communication is directly between the device and your organisation's Jamf School instance.

Data Storage and Security

SABSorter keeps no account data and runs no servers. It reads the configuration provided by your MDM and may hold transient, non-secret diagnostics on-device (for example, configuration validation results and the time of the last refresh) to help administrators troubleshoot. Secret values are never displayed. All network communication uses HTTPS only, and the app runs within the iOS application sandbox.

Your Organisation's Role

Your school or organisation operates the Jamf School instance and decides which devices, locations, users, and credentials are configured. With respect to that data, your organisation is the data controller, and its own privacy notice and data-processing terms apply. SABSorter acts only as a client to that system; Your IT Solution does not process your data on any server it controls.

Data Retention and Deletion

SABSorter stores nothing on the developer's side, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Removing the credentials from the configuration returns the app to its zero-secret default; removing the app ends all access. Records of a device's location reside in your organisation's Jamf School instance and are controlled by your organisation.

Children's Privacy

SABSorter is a device-administration tool for organisation-managed, shared iPads. It does not require account creation and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Any user information it reads (such as a staff username used to pre-select a location) comes from your organisation's existing Jamf School directory, under your organisation's control, and is not collected by the developer.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated date. Because SABSorter collects no contact information, we cannot notify users directly; we encourage periodic review.
