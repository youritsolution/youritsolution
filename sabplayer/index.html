What It Does

Bring your whole library together without copying a single file.

10-band Parametric equalizer with 12 built-in presets and custom saves.
1-12 sec Configurable dual-deck crossfade for seamless transitions.
0.5x-2.0x Playback speed control with pitch preservation for music and spoken audio.

Play From Anywhere

Point SAB Audio at the files you already have.

Import individual files or entire folders from any storage provider that appears in the iOS Files app. SAB Audio creates a lightweight reference to each file and plays it in place, so your phone storage stays free.

Studio-Grade Equalizer

Shape your sound with precision.

Choose from Rock, Pop, Jazz, Classical, Bass Boost, and more, or create and save your own presets with a professional 10-band parametric equalizer.

Dual-Deck Crossfade

Keep the music moving.

Seamless transitions between tracks with a configurable crossfade engine from 1 to 12 seconds. No gaps, no silence, no awkward handoff between songs.

Playlists & Sessions

Pick up exactly where you left off.

Create playlists, reorder tracks, and save entire playback sessions including queue, position, EQ settings, speed, and shuffle state.

Smart Library

Find the right track faster.

Your library organizes itself into Continue Listening, Recently Added, Recently Played, Most Played, and tracks that need attention. Browse by artist or album and detect duplicates.

Bookmarks & Resume

Made for long listening.

Mark positions in long tracks for audiobooks, lectures, and podcasts. SAB Audio remembers where you stopped and offers to resume when you come back.

Playback Speed & Sleep Timer

Listen on your schedule.

Go from 0.5x to 2.0x with pitch preservation, switch automatically for spoken audio, and use preset or custom timers with a visual countdown that pauses playback when time runs out.

AirPlay, Background, Accessibility

Fits naturally into iPhone and iPad listening.

Send audio to HomePod, AirPods, Bluetooth speakers, Apple TV, or any AirPlay-compatible device. Control playback from the Lock Screen, Control Center, or headphone buttons, with VoiceOver support, haptic feedback, and a dark interface designed for comfortable listening.

Formats

Bring in the formats you actually use.

SAB Audio supports M4A, MP3, AAC, FLAC, WAV, AIFF, OGG, Opus, CAF, ALAC, and any format supported by Apple's audio framework.
