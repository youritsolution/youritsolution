Your premium iOS audio player for files that stay where they are.

SAB Audio plays your audio files directly from iCloud Drive, device storage, and any provider that appears in the iOS Files app. No file duplication, no wasted space, no account required. Just point, play, and enjoy.

Why It Wins

Sharper for direct-file listening than the usual cloud-player tradeoffs.

Direct Playback

Keep your library where it already lives.

Many competing iOS players lean on downloading, syncing, or rebuilding a second managed library. SAB Audio is centered on playing files in place through the iOS Files workflow, which is faster to set up and easier to live with.

Storage-Friendly

No duplication means no wasted device space.

Instead of copying your music into the app, SAB Audio keeps lightweight references to the original files. That makes it especially strong for larger collections, cloud-backed libraries, and long-form audio.

Listening Continuity

Sessions, bookmarks, resume, and spoken-audio awareness.

SAB Audio goes beyond basic playback with saved sessions, resume position memory, track bookmarks, configurable skip intervals, and automatic speed behavior for spoken content.

Private By Design

No account wall between you and your music.

No ads. No tracking. No account required. SAB Audio is designed around your files and your device, not around collecting usage data or pushing you into a cloud subscription loop.

Core Experience

The features that make everyday listening feel thought through.

Play From Anywhere

Import files or whole folders from the Files app.

Bring in individual tracks or entire folders from iCloud Drive, On My iPhone, and connected storage providers. SAB Audio reads metadata, artwork, title, artist, album, genre, track number, and duration without moving the original file.

Now Playing

Built for touch, glance, and control.

The Now Playing screen gives you artwork, queue source, precise scrubber control, content-type switching, shuffle, repeat, configurable skip controls, volume, and a feature grid for queue, EQ, speed, sleep timer, AirPlay, bookmarks, and playlist actions.

Studio-Grade Equalizer

Shape your sound with precision.

Use a professional 10-band parametric equalizer with gain control from -12 dB to +12 dB in 0.5 dB steps. Choose from 12 built-in presets or save your own custom presets permanently.

Dual-Deck Crossfade

Keep the music moving.

SAB Audio uses a dual-deck audio engine for seamless transitions between tracks, with configurable crossfade duration from 1 to 12 seconds or fully off when you want exact track boundaries.

Playlists & Sessions

Return to the exact state you left.

Create playlists, reorder tracks, save queue state as sessions, and restore queue, position, EQ settings, playback rate, repeat mode, and shuffle state in one tap.

Smart Library

Surface the tracks that matter right now.

Browse All, Favorites, Artists, and Albums, then let smart collections surface Continue Listening, Recently Added, Recently Played, Most Played, Needs Attention, and Potential Duplicates automatically.

Bookmarks & Resume

Especially good for long-form audio.

Create named bookmarks inside tracks, save resume positions automatically, and switch content type between Music and Spoken Audio when you want the app to behave differently.

Playback Speed & Sleep Timer

Listen on your schedule.

Go from 0.5x to 2.0x with pitch preservation, switch automatically for spoken audio, and use preset or custom timers with a visual countdown that pauses playback when time runs out.

AirPlay & Background Playback

Move naturally across devices and contexts.

Send audio to HomePod, AirPods, Bluetooth speakers, Apple TV, and other AirPlay-compatible devices, while keeping playback controls available from the Lock Screen, Control Center, and headphone buttons.

Accessibility

Comfortable to use, not just feature-rich.

SAB Audio includes VoiceOver support, labeled playback controls, adjustable EQ bands for assistive workflows, haptic feedback on key actions, and a dark interface designed for comfortable listening.

Formats

Bring in the formats you actually use.

SAB Audio supports M4A, MP3, AAC, FLAC, WAV, AIFF, AIFC, OGG Vorbis, Opus, CAF, ALAC, WMA, and any format supported by Apple's AVFoundation framework.
