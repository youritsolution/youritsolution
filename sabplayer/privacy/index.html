Overview

SAB Audio is an audio player for iOS that plays audio files stored on your device or in cloud storage services such as iCloud Drive. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data SAB Audio accesses, how it is used, and what it does not do.

The short version: SAB Audio does not collect, transmit, or share any of your personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Data SAB Audio Accesses

Audio Files

SAB Audio accesses audio files that you explicitly select through the iOS file picker or folder picker. The app creates security-scoped bookmarks to reference these files so they can be played without copying them into the app's storage. SAB Audio never accesses files you have not selected.

Audio Metadata

When you import audio files, SAB Audio reads embedded metadata (title, artist, album name, album artwork, genre, track number, and duration) to display track information in the app. This metadata is stored locally on your device in the app's database.

Playback Data

SAB Audio stores the following playback-related data locally on your device:

  • Play count and last played date for each track
  • Resume position for long-form audio (audiobooks, podcasts)
  • Playlists and their track order
  • Playback sessions (saved queue states)
  • Position bookmarks within tracks
  • Equalizer presets and preferences
  • Sleep timer and playback speed preferences
  • Favorite track selections

All of this data is stored exclusively on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework and UserDefaults. None of it is transmitted to any server.

Data SAB Audio Does NOT Collect

SAB Audio does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, or any personal identification information
  • Your location or GPS data
  • Your contacts, photos, or calendar data
  • Device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • Usage analytics or telemetry
  • Crash reports (no third-party crash reporting services are used)
  • Any data to third-party services, advertisers, or analytics providers

Network Access

SAB Audio does not make any network requests. The app operates entirely offline. The only network activity that may occur is initiated by iOS itself when accessing files stored in iCloud Drive (managed by Apple's iCloud framework, not by SAB Audio).

Third-Party Services

SAB Audio does not integrate any third-party services, SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or tracking frameworks. The app contains no third-party code that collects data.

iCloud Drive

When you select files from iCloud Drive, SAB Audio uses Apple's security-scoped bookmark API to maintain access to those files. The file data remains in iCloud Drive under your Apple ID. SAB Audio does not upload, modify, or delete your iCloud Drive files. SAB Audio may trigger a download of iCloud files that exist only as cloud placeholders, using Apple's standard file management APIs.

AirPlay

When you use AirPlay to stream audio to external devices, the audio data is transmitted directly from your device to the AirPlay receiver using Apple's built-in AirPlay framework. SAB Audio does not intercept, log, or transmit this data through any other channel.

Data Storage and Security

All data stored by SAB Audio resides on your device and is protected by iOS's built-in security features, including:

  • App sandboxing (SAB Audio cannot access data from other apps)
  • Device encryption (data at rest is encrypted when your device is locked)
  • Security-scoped bookmarks (file access is limited to files you explicitly grant)

Data Retention and Deletion

All data stored by SAB Audio can be deleted at any time by:

  • Removing individual tracks from the Library
  • Using "Remove All from Library" in Settings
  • Deleting the SAB Audio app from your device (this removes all app data)

Deleting tracks from SAB Audio's library does not delete the original audio files from iCloud Drive or your device storage.

Children's Privacy

SAB Audio does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app does not require account creation or any form of registration.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated date. Since SAB Audio does not collect any contact information, we cannot notify users directly of changes. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Your Rights

Since SAB Audio does not collect or store any personal data on external servers, there is no personal data to request, export, or delete from our end. All your data is on your device and under your control.
