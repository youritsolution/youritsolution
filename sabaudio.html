SAB Audio

Your music and audiobooks, played straight from iCloud Drive — without copies on your device.

SAB Audio is a hi-fi player for iPhone and iPad that references the audio files you already own. Bit-perfect playback, audiobook chapters, smart playlists, sleep timer, EBU R128 loudness, per-headphone EQ memory, listening history, and multi-select bulk actions — built for people with serious libraries and zero patience for cloud lock-in.

Built for serious listeners

Sonic credibility, not just a long feature list.

SAB Audio plays your files at their native sample rate, with a Pure Mode toggle that bypasses every DSP stage so what reaches your DAC is exactly what's on disk. Loudness is measured to the EBU R128 standard, not a heuristic. Crossfade is sample-accurate. Same-album tracks transition gaplessly when they should and crossfade when they shouldn't.

  • Pure Mode — bit-perfect output, every effect bypassed at the engine level
  • Sample-rate matching to the connected output, including USB DACs over Lightning or USB-C
  • EBU R128 K-weighted loudness analysis — proper measurement, not a heuristic
  • Same-album gapless heuristic; configurable crossfade up to 30 seconds otherwise
  • Format pill on Now Playing surfaces what you're actually hearing

For audiobooks and long-form audio

The player remembers what kind of audio you're listening to.

Music tracks start at zero. Audiobooks resume where you left off. Spoken-word automatically switches to your spoken-content speed (1.25× by default). Smart Speed quietly trims the silences in podcasts. Sleep timer fades out instead of cutting off. Chapter navigation works on m4b files and on chaptered m4a recordings.

  • Auto-detection of long-form content — m4b files always, anything over your threshold otherwise
  • Per-content playback speed — 1× for music, 1.25× for spoken
  • Sleep timer modes: minutes, end of current track, end of queue, with audio fade-out
  • Chapter list with prev / next buttons, and a chapter chip in Now Playing

A library that scales

Built and tested with libraries of 4,000+ tracks.

Smart playlists update themselves from rules you define. Multi-select bulk actions queue, playlist, session, favorite, pin offline, or remove anything you've ticked. Folder browsing matches your iCloud Drive structure exactly. Find Duplicates, Repair Broken Links, Tag Editor, M3U import — library hygiene that respects your time.

  • Smart playlists by genre, play count, days since played, favorite status, content type
  • Multi-select with bulk Play Now, Shuffle, Play Next, Add to Queue, Save as Playlist, Save as Session, Pin Offline
  • Folder hierarchy mirroring iCloud Drive — recursive selection, drill-down navigation
  • Find Duplicates by title + artist match, with one-tap removal
  • Repair Broken Links — re-bind a moved file without losing play count or playlist membership
  • Tag Editor with user-edit flag that survives metadata refresh
  • M3U / M3U8 export and import for interop with other players

DSP and output

Per-output EQ, Replay Gain, and route-aware audio session handling.

  • 10-band parametric equalizer with 12 built-in presets and unlimited custom presets
  • Per-output-route EQ memory — your AirPods curve is not your car's curve
  • Bass Boost, A-B Loop, Smart Speed (silence trimming for podcasts and spoken word)
  • Replay Gain analysis (offline, on-device) and per-track or album-mode gain application
  • Audio session activates only when playback starts — never disrupts the car's FM radio on launch
  • Lock screen, Control Center, Siri Shortcuts, AirPlay, CarPlay (where supported)

Honest privacy

Nothing tracks you. Everything stays on your device.

SAB Audio collects nothing. There are no analytics SDKs, no advertising frameworks, no crash reporters, and no third-party trackers. Two optional integrations — MusicBrainz metadata enrichment and Last.fm scrobbling — are off by default and only send the metadata you'd expect those services to receive. You can disable either at any time in Settings.

Support & contact

Need help? Email the developer.

SAB Audio is built and maintained by an independent developer. Bug reports, feature requests, and questions are welcome.

Email: support@youritsolution.dk

When reporting a bug, please include your iPhone or iPad model, the iOS version, and a one-line description of what you did and what you expected to happen. That's all we need.

Quick answers

  • "My track has no artist or album." The file likely lacks embedded metadata. Use the Tag Editor to enter values manually, or enable MusicBrainz enrichment in Settings.
  • "A track says 'Needs Repair' or won't play." The original file may have moved or been renamed in iCloud Drive. Use Settings → Repair Broken Links to reconnect references without losing play count or playlist membership.
  • "Can I listen offline?" Yes — long-press a track or use multi-select → Pin for Offline. iCloud Drive will download the file and Settings → Offline Downloads shows progress.
  • "Bit-perfect playback?" Enable Pure Mode in Settings. Connect a USB DAC via Lightning or USB-C and SAB Audio will request the source's native sample rate.
