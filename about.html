Background

Enterprise IT, Apple platforms, and Microsoft infrastructure.

Apple-focused

I have been working with Apple platforms in enterprise environments for years. That experience shapes how I build tools — native macOS first, no compromises on the experience just because it is an admin app.

Microsoft and Intune

Managing Microsoft environments — Active Directory, Windows deployments, and now modern Intune and Entra tenants — is a large part of my professional background. Intune Companion grew directly out of that day-to-day work.

Virtualization and infrastructure

Testing and staging environments matter. VirtualMac Studio came from wanting a clean, native way to spin up macOS VMs on Apple silicon without fighting third-party abstractions.

Education and early training

Formal IT education at IT-Akademiet covering hardware, software, networking, Java programming, object-oriented analysis and design, and project management — followed by two decades of hands-on enterprise experience.

Why I Build This

Tools I needed, shared so others do not have to start from scratch.

Scratch your own itch

Every app here started as something I personally needed. I ran into a frustration, wrote something to fix it, and then made it properly releasable so it could help the next person who hits the same wall.

Always free

This is hobby work, not a business. I am not trying to monetize admin pain. The tools are free and I intend to keep them that way.
