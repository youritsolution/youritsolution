SAB Audio

Privacy Policy

SAB Audio is designed to keep your data on your device. The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. This policy covers both the iOS version (iPhone & iPad) and the Android version. The only outbound network calls are optional integrations that you must explicitly enable.

Last updated: May 16, 2026

Audio Files

SAB Audio accesses audio files that you explicitly select through the system file or folder picker. On iOS, the app creates security-scoped bookmarks. On Android, the app uses the Storage Access Framework (SAF) and persists read permission only for the folder or files you grant. In both cases the files are referenced in place and not copied. SAB Audio never accesses files you have not selected.

Audio Metadata

When you import audio files, SAB Audio reads embedded metadata (title, artist, album, artwork, genre, track number, duration) to display track information. This metadata is stored locally on your device in the app's database.

Playback Data

SAB Audio stores the following on your device only: play count, last played date, resume positions for long-form audio, playlists and their order, saved playback sessions, position bookmarks, equalizer presets and preferences, sleep timer and playback speed defaults, and favorite track selections.

What SAB Audio Does Not Collect

SAB Audio does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, calendar data, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, usage analytics, or crash reports. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking frameworks.

Android Permissions

The Android version of SAB Audio requests only the permissions strictly necessary for local audio playback:

  • Read access to user-selected folders or files via the Storage Access Framework (no broad READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE permission is requested)
  • Foreground service of type mediaPlayback — required by Android so playback can continue while the app is in the background or the screen is off
  • Post notifications — required on Android 13+ to show the playback notification with play/pause/skip controls on the lock screen and in the notification shade
  • Wake lock — keeps the CPU awake during active playback so audio doesn't stutter when the screen turns off
  • Internet — used only by the optional MusicBrainz and Last.fm integrations described below; no other network requests are made

The app does not request access to your microphone, camera, contacts, calendar, location, phone state, SMS, call logs, or any device identifier. It does not include any advertising ID lookup.

Network Access

The app operates almost entirely offline. The only outbound network requests are:

  • iCloud Drive file access (iOS only) — managed by Apple's iCloud framework, not by SAB Audio
  • MusicBrainz metadata enrichment — off by default. When enabled, the app sends track titles and artist names to musicbrainz.org to fetch missing metadata. No personal data is sent
  • Last.fm scrobbling — off by default. When you sign in, the app sends track title, artist, album, duration, and timestamp to last.fm. Authentication uses Last.fm's session-key flow; SAB Audio never sees or stores your Last.fm password

Both integrations can be disabled at any time in Settings. The Android version makes no network requests at all unless you explicitly enable one of these two integrations.

iCloud Drive (iOS only)

When you select files from iCloud Drive, SAB Audio uses Apple's security-scoped bookmark API to maintain access. File data remains in iCloud Drive under your Apple ID. SAB Audio does not upload, modify, or delete your iCloud Drive files. The app may trigger a download of files that exist only as cloud placeholders, using Apple's standard file management APIs.

On-Device Storage (Android)

On Android, SAB Audio reads from a folder or set of files you explicitly grant via the system Storage Access Framework picker. The app persists the granted permission so the folder is still readable across launches, but the permission can be revoked at any time from the Android system settings. SAB Audio does not move, copy, modify, or delete your audio files. Files imported from cloud-backed providers (Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, etc.) are accessed through the same SAF mechanism — SAB Audio never sees your cloud account credentials.

Data Storage and Security

All data resides on your device. On iOS, it is protected by app sandboxing, device encryption when locked, and security-scoped bookmarks. On Android, it is protected by the per-app private data directory, SAF persisted permissions scoped to the folder you granted, and the device's file-based encryption. The app does not back up its database to iCloud, Google Drive, or any other cloud service.

Data Retention and Deletion

All data stored by SAB Audio can be deleted at any time by removing individual tracks, using "Remove All from Library" in Settings, or uninstalling the app. Deleting tracks from SAB Audio does not delete the original audio files from your storage (iCloud Drive, internal storage, SD card, or the SAF-granted folder). On Android, uninstalling the app also revokes any SAF permissions you granted.

Children's Privacy

SAB Audio does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app does not require account creation or registration.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated date. Since SAB Audio does not collect contact information, we cannot notify you directly. We encourage periodic review.
