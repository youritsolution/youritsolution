Dialog Architect

Privacy Policy

Dialog Architect is designed to be local-first where possible. It stores project data on your Mac, uses the macOS Keychain for sensitive secrets, and only talks to external services when the workflow itself requires it.

What You Put Into The App

Dialog Architect can store project content such as titles, messages, branding URLs, colors, action definitions, user entry fields, templates, identifiers, version numbers, runtime settings, and export preferences.

Local Storage

Projects, templates, previews, and exported files are created and stored on your Mac in locations you choose or that the app manages locally. The website does not receive this data automatically.

Keychain and Secrets

When you configure Jamf Pro integration, the client secret should be stored in the macOS Keychain. Signing identities are discovered from your Keychain, and notarization workflows depend on credentials you set up on your own Mac.

Jamf Pro Integration

If you connect Dialog Architect to Jamf Pro, the app communicates directly with the Jamf Pro server URL you configure. This may include browsing resources, importing profiles, fetching departments or buildings, and uploading or updating profiles.

Exports and Deployments

When you export a JSM mobileconfig or a swiftDialog package, the resulting file is written to disk where you choose. If you upload a profile to Jamf Pro, the generated profile data is sent to your Jamf Pro environment as part of that action.

Diagnostics and Logs

Dialog Architect can produce logs or diagnostics that help with troubleshooting. If you choose to send them to support, review them first for tenant details, server URLs, package identifiers, image URLs, or other information you do not want to share.

No Advertising Tracking

Dialog Architect is not built around ad SDKs, cross-app profiling, or selling your data. The product is intended as an admin tool, not an advertising business.

Beta Note

Because Dialog Architect is currently in invite-only beta, support and feedback may happen directly by email rather than through a public community hub. That does not change the privacy principles above.
