SAB Enroll Hub

Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 5, 2026

Overview

SAB Enroll Hub is a Mac-native administration tool for Apple Business Manager and Apple School Manager device enrollment workflows.

Data Stored On Your Mac

SAB Enroll Hub stores organization names, Client IDs, Team IDs, Key IDs, local device cache data, sync metrics, and audit logs on your Mac so the app can reopen organizations quickly and provide fast indexed search and diagnostics.

Private Keys and Credentials

Private key PEM material is stored in the macOS Keychain and is not written to disk in plain text by the app. API tokens are kept in memory only and are not persisted to disk.

Device Cache, Metrics, and Logs

Device inventory cache, sync metrics, and audit information are stored locally so the app can provide fast lookup, cross-portal search, diagnostics, and change-tracking support.

Log files are stored on your Mac and can be opened through the app's logs folder command.

Network Use

SAB Enroll Hub uses HTTPS connections to Apple's Apple Business Manager and Apple School Manager API endpoints. The app requires network access because its core purpose is to query and manage enrollment data from those services.

User-Selected Files

If you export CSV files or diagnostics archives, the app writes those files only to locations you choose. You control whether you export them and where they are saved.

Diagnostics and Support

If you contact support, you may choose to send screenshots, logs, or diagnostics archives manually. You decide what information to share.

Analytics and Tracking

SAB Enroll Hub is not built around third-party advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking, or selling user data.
